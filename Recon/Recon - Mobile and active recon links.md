# Recon - Mobile and active recon links

**TL;DR:** Android options for scanning, plus reference links for active recon.

Mobile:
- WiFiAnalyzer (open source, no internet permission): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vrem.wifianalyzer
- Fing (network and port scanner): https://www.fing.io
- Kali NetHunter: https://www.kali.org/docs/nethunter/ and https://www.kali.org/docs/nethunter-pro/

Practice target: Metasploitable2 from Rapid7 (SourceForge), on a host-only or internal network only. Never bridge it.

Other tools and references: https://github.com/ThomasHabets/arping, https://www.grc.com/shieldsup (test your own exposure), https://pentest-tools.com, http://blindelephant.sourceforge.net (web app version fingerprinting), https://www.kali.org/tools/, https://www.cyberciti.biz/security/nmap-command-examples-tutorials/, https://www.iana.org/assignments/ipv6-multicast-addresses/ipv6-multicast-addresses.xhtml
