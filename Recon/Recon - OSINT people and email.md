# Recon - OSINT people and email

**TL;DR:** Organisation to names, names plus email format to addresses, address or username to accounts. Every username hit needs corroboration.

1. Names and roles: company site, annual reports, press, job postings (they leak the tech stack), `site:linkedin.com/in "<ORG>"`, public registers (Belgium: Staatsblad, KBO/BCE).
2. Automate names: [[Tool - CrossLinked]].
3. Email format and addresses: https://hunter.io, [[Tool - theHarvester]], [[Tool - emailharvester]].
4. Address to services: [[Tool - Holehe]] or https://epieos.com (not passive).
5. Username pivots: [[Tool - Sherlock]], [[Tool - Maigret]], https://whatsmyname.app
