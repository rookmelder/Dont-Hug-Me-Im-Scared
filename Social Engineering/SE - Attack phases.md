# SE - Attack phases

**TL;DR:** An SE engagement runs in four phases: **Research → Hook → Play → Exit**. Most of the win is in research; the exit matters because you want to leave no trace the target can act on.

from: SE - Social Engineering course doc

## The phases

1. **Research / Reconnaissance** — gather everything on the target (person or org): public docs, website, social media, dumpster diving, OSINT. This is where the pretext is built. Good recon is the difference between a working and a failed pretext.
2. **Hook / Engagement** — make first contact and open the interaction.
3. **Play / Exploitation** — build rapport, strengthen the connection, and extract the information or action you're after.
4. **Exit / Closure** — leave cleanly without raising suspicion, so the target doesn't realise what happened or trace it back.

A similar four-step framing is the social-engineer.org attack cycle.
