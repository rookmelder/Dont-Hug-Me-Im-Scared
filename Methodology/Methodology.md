# Methodology

Back to [[Home]].

The stages of an attack in order, as checklists. Follow them top to bottom; each links to the topic notes that explain the steps. The matching note label is `phase:` (see `CLAUDE.md`).

## Stages

1. [[Method - 1 Recon]]: find hosts and open services
2. [[Method - 2 Enumeration]]: dig into each service for a way in
3. [[Method - 3 Initial access]]: turn a finding into a foothold
4. [[Method - 4 Privilege escalation]]: foothold user → root / SYSTEM
5. [[Method - 5 Lateral movement]]: reach other hosts and the domain
6. [[Method - 6 Persistence & cleanup]]: keep access, collect evidence, tidy up
